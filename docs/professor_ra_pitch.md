# Project Pitch for RA / PhD Conversations

I am interested in information opacity and valuation in private credit markets. As a pilot project, I constructed a borrower-level valuation panel from BDC Schedule of Investments disclosures for ARCC, BXSL, and OBDC, tracking Q4 2025 to Q1 2026 changes in fair value-to-cost ratios. I then designed a public information visibility screen to examine whether valuation markdowns were accompanied by observable borrower-level deterioration signals.

The goal is not to claim that public news predicts private credit marks. Instead, I use public information as an outside-investor information set. In the pilot sample, several markdown events are not accompanied by obvious borrower-specific hard-credit signals in public sources. Most identifiable signals are industry or comparable-company level, which motivates a broader research question about how and when credit deterioration becomes observable in private debt markets.

A natural extension would scale the dataset across more BDCs and quarters, apply multiple markdown thresholds, and connect valuation marks to public-source visibility, non-accrual status, restructurings, and industry conditions.
