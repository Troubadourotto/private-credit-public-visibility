# RA Email Blurb

I have started a small pilot project on information opacity and credit-risk discovery in private debt markets. The project constructs a borrower-level panel of BDC-held private credit exposures from Schedule of Investments disclosures for ARCC, BXSL, and OBDC, tracks Q4 2025 to Q1 2026 changes in fair value-to-cost ratios, and codes whether valuation markdown cases are accompanied by borrower-specific public deterioration signals, industry/comparable context, or no clear public signal. The goal is not to make a causal or predictive claim, but to demonstrate a scalable research design for studying whether BDC valuation marks reveal borrower-level deterioration that is difficult for outside investors to observe from public information alone. I would be very interested in contributing to projects involving private credit, direct lending, BDCs, valuation opacity, non-bank lending, or credit-risk data construction.
